## 1. Multi-Framework Compliance Gap Analysis (PCI DSS v4.0, SOC 2, HIPAA)

**Type:** ![Self-Assessment](https://img.shields.io/badge/type-Self--Assessment-6a737d) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Status:** ![Completed](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Completed-28a745) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Findings:** 5  
**Lead:** Augustine Ozor &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Dates:** 2026-09-14 → 2026-10-09

**Scope**  
Assess 15 controls across PCI DSS v4.0, SOC 2 Type II, and HIPAA against the platform as it runs today, rate each control's effectiveness, and consolidate the results into a prioritized issue list. Output: 5 issues and the treatment plan that feeds the remediation roadmap.
